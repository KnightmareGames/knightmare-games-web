Description

Your mouse cursor has spent enough time just clicking on things. It wants to do more. It wants to adventure!

Cursor Quest is a single-player action RPG where you play as your cursor. Swing weapons, defeat monsters, gather resources, craft gear, find epic and rare equipment, and work your way through dozens of handcrafted regions.

Features

Put your mouse skills to the test

Your cursor movements drive your positioning and your weapons. Build momentum for a strike, move clear of an incoming attack, and cast powerful spells and incantations. All with a little flick of the wrist.

Build your cursor

Go sword and shield, swing a heavy two-handed weapon, or start throwing spells around. Mix in capes, prayers, pets, and rare gear to find combinations that fit your playstyle. There's a loadout for everyone out there.

Real skill progression

Swing an axe to chop trees, mine ore with your pickaxe, and cast a line to catch fish. Cook your catch for the next fight and turn your ore into new gear. Your skills grow as you use them, unlocking better resources and equipment.

Find your next adventure

Head into handcrafted regions full of monsters, resources, and things worth wandering off the path for. Venture into dungeons and take on bosses that put your build and your skills to the test, in a living, changing world full of rare discoveries.
